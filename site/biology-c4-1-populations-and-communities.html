Populations and quadrat sampling

A population is a group of organisms of the same species living in the same area at the same time, interacting (e.g. interbreeding). Counting every individual is usually impossible, so we sample randomly and estimate.

🌼 Quadrat sampling game

The field is 20 m × 12 m (240 m²). Each quadrat is 1 m². How many daisies are there?

  • Use random coordinates (random number generator) to place quadrats, avoiding bias.
  • Estimated population = mean number per quadrat × (total area ÷ quadrat area).
  • Sampling error: the estimate may differ from the true value; more samples reduce it.

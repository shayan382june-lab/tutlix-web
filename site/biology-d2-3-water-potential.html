⚡ D2.3 in 60 seconds

  1. Water dissolves polar solutes by solvation.
  2. Osmosis: water moves to higher solute concentration across a partially permeable membrane.
  3. Animal cells burst/crenate; plant cells become turgid or plasmolysed.
  4. Find the isotonic point where % mass change = 0.
  5. Isotonic solutions are used for IV drips and organ transplants.
  6. HL: ψ = ψs + ψp; water moves from higher to lower ψ.

D2.3.1 – D2.3.3

Solutes and osmosis

  • Water is an excellent solvent: polar water molecules form hydrogen bonds with polar and charged solutes (solvation), surrounding them in a hydration shell.
  • Osmosis: the net movement of water across a partially permeable membrane from a region of lower solute concentration to higher solute concentration.
  • This is because solute molecules attract water molecules (hydrogen bonds), reducing the water free to move.
  • Solutions compared to a cell: hypotonic (lower solute concentration), isotonic (equal), hypertonic (higher).

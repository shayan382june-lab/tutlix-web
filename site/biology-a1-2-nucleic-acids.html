⚡ A1.2 in 60 seconds

  1. DNA is the genetic material of all living things (some viruses use RNA).
  2. Nucleotides = phosphate + pentose sugar + base, joined by condensation.
  3. DNA: double helix, antiparallel strands, A–T and C–G pairs.
  4. RNA: single strand, ribose, U instead of T.
  5. Complementary pairing allows copying; 4n sequences; universal code.
  6. HL: 5′→3′ · purine + pyrimidine · nucleosomes · Hershey–Chase · Chargaff.

A1.2.1

DNA: the genetic material

The instructions for building and running every living organism are stored in DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid), passed from parent to offspring and from cell to cell.

  • All living organisms (bacteria, archaea, plants, animals, fungi) use DNA as their genetic material.
  • Some viruses (e.g. influenza, HIV) use RNA instead. Viruses are not considered living.

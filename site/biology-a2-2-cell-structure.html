⚡ A2.2 in 60 seconds

  1. The cell theory: all life is made of cells, which come from pre-existing cells.
  2. Magnification = image ÷ actual size; electron microscopes have far better resolution.
  3. All cells have DNA, cytoplasm, a plasma membrane and ribosomes.
  4. Prokaryotes: no nucleus, naked DNA loop, 70S ribosomes. Eukaryotes: nucleus, organelles, 80S ribosomes.
  5. Plants have cellulose walls, chloroplasts and big vacuoles; fungi have chitin walls; animals have centrioles.
  6. HL: mitochondria and chloroplasts came from endosymbiosis; cells differentiate by gene expression.

A2.2.1

Cells: the basic unit of life

The cell theory states that:

  • All living organisms are made of one or more cells.
  • The cell is the smallest unit of life.
  • Cells only come from pre-existing cells by cell division.

The theory came from observations with microscopes, and has been tested by looking at huge numbers of organisms. It is a good example of inductive reasoning: a general rule drawn from many observations.

🔬 Nature of science

Some cells don't fit neatly, like muscle fibres with many nuclei (section A2.2.9). Scientists keep the theory because it explains far more than these exceptions challenge.

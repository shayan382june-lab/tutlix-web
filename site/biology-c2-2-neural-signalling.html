Neurons and the resting potential

Neurons carry electrical impulses. They have a cell body, dendrites that receive signals, and a long axon (nerve fibre) that carries impulses away, often wrapped in myelin.

  • At rest the inside of the axon is about −70 mV relative to outside: the resting potential.
  • The Na⁺/K⁺ pump uses ATP to pump 3 Na⁺ out for every 2 K⁺ in, creating concentration gradients.
  • The membrane is more permeable to K⁺ (leak channels), so K⁺ diffuses out, leaving the inside negative. Negatively charged proteins inside add to this.

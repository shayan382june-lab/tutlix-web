⚡ D1.1 in 60 seconds

  1. Replication makes identical copies of DNA.
  2. It is semi-conservative: each new molecule = 1 old + 1 new strand (Meselson & Stahl).
  3. Helicase unzips; DNA polymerase builds by complementary base pairing.
  4. PCR amplifies DNA; gel electrophoresis separates fragments by size.
  5. HL: 5′→3′ only: leading strand continuous, lagging strand in Okazaki fragments.
  6. HL: primase, Pol III, Pol I, ligase; proofreading.

D1.1.1 – D1.1.3

Copying DNA

  • DNA replication produces exact copies of DNA with identical base sequences, needed for growth, tissue repair and reproduction (before every cell division).
  • It is semi-conservative: each new molecule has one strand from the original (conserved) and one newly built strand.
  • Complementary base pairing (A–T, C–G) means each old strand acts as a template that determines the sequence of the new strand.
  • Helicase unwinds the double helix and separates the strands by breaking hydrogen bonds.
  • DNA polymerase links free nucleotides to form the new strand, following the template.

⚡ D1.3 in 60 seconds

  1. Mutations: substitution, insertion, deletion.
  2. Substitutions can be silent, missense or nonsense; insertions/deletions cause frameshifts.
  3. Mutations are random; mutagens (radiation, chemicals) raise the rate.
  4. Somatic → cancer, not inherited; germ-line → inherited.
  5. Mutation is the source of genetic variation.

D1.3.1 – D1.3.3

Gene mutations

  • A gene mutation is a change to the base sequence of a gene.
  • Point mutations: substitution (one base replaced), insertion or deletion of one or more bases.
  • Effects of a substitution: silent (same amino acid, thanks to the degenerate code), missense (different amino acid, e.g. sickle cell), nonsense (early STOP codon).
  • Insertions/deletions cause a frameshift unless a multiple of 3 bases is added/removed: every codon downstream changes.
  • Block mutations (larger changes) also occur, e.g. duplications or deletions of whole sections.

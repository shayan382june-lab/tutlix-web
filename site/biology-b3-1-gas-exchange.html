⚡ B3.1 in 60 seconds

  1. Exchange surfaces: permeable, thin, moist, large; gradients kept by blood flow and ventilation.
  2. Lungs: alveoli, surfactant, capillaries; ventilation uses antagonistic muscles and pressure changes.
  3. Spirometers measure tidal volume, vital capacity and reserves.
  4. Leaves: stomata, air spaces, mesophyll; transpiration is a side effect of gas exchange.
  5. HL: cooperative binding gives a sigmoid curve.
  6. HL: fetal Hb has higher affinity; the Bohr shift releases O₂ in respiring tissues.

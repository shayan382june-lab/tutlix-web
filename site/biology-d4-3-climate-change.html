⚡ D4.3 in 60 seconds

  1. Human emissions of CO₂ and methane enhance the greenhouse effect.
  2. Positive feedbacks (ice–albedo, permafrost) accelerate warming; tipping points.
  3. Effects: polar habitat loss, weaker upwelling, range shifts.
  4. Corals: bleaching and ocean acidification.
  5. Carbon sequestration: forests, peat, wetlands.
  6. HL: phenology mismatches, insect outbreaks, evolution.

D4.3.1 – D4.3.2

Causes of climate change

  • Anthropogenic (human) climate change is caused mainly by increased concentrations of greenhouse gases: CO₂ from burning fossil fuels and deforestation, methane from cattle, rice paddies, landfill and leaks, and nitrous oxide from fertilisers.
  • Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit long-wave infrared radiation, warming the lower atmosphere and surface.
  • Positive feedback amplifies warming: ice–albedo effect, thawing permafrost releasing CO₂ and methane, more forest fires, warmer oceans absorbing less CO₂.
  • Tipping points: e.g. boreal forest or Amazon dieback, collapse of ice sheets.

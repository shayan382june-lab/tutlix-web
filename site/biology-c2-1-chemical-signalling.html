⚡ C2.1 in 60 seconds

  1. Ligands bind specific receptors.
  2. Bacteria use quorum sensing (Vibrio fischeri glows when dense).
  3. Hormones, neurotransmitters, cytokines and Ca²⁺ act at different ranges.
  4. Hydrophilic → transmembrane receptors; hydrophobic steroids → intracellular receptors.
  5. Adrenaline → G protein → cAMP cascade: huge amplification; insulin → tyrosine kinase → GLUT4.
  6. Signalling is regulated by negative and positive feedback.

Receptors and quorum sensing

Cells communicate with chemical signals (ligands). A ligand binds to a receptor: a protein with a binding site specific to that ligand. Binding changes the receptor's shape and triggers a response.

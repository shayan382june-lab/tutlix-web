⚡ A4.2 in 60 seconds

  1. Biodiversity has three levels: genetic, species and ecosystem.
  2. About 8.7 million eukaryote species exist; more than 99% of all species ever have gone extinct.
  3. Humans caused extinctions such as the moa, Caribbean monk seal and (in the wild) the toromiro.
  4. Evidence: IUCN Red List, Living Planet Index (−73% since 1970), citizen science.
  5. Causes: HIPPO + climate change.
  6. Conservation uses in situ, ex situ and rewilding; EDGE species get priority.

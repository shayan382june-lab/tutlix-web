⚡ A1.1 in 60 seconds

  1. Water is polar: O is δ−, each H is δ+.
  2. Polarity causes hydrogen bonds between water molecules: weak alone, strong in huge numbers.
  3. H-bonds give cohesion (xylem transport, surface tension) and adhesion (capillary action).
  4. Water dissolves polar and charged substances, so it is the medium for metabolism and transport.
  5. Compared with air, water has more buoyancy, viscosity, thermal conductivity and specific heat capacity.
  6. HL: Earth's water probably came from asteroids; liquid water is the first thing we look for in the search for life.
A1.1.1

Water as the medium for life

The first cells most likely arose in water, and water is still the medium in which all life's chemistry happens. Cytoplasm is mostly water, and most metabolic reactions take place between molecules dissolved in it.

  • Dissolved molecules can move and collide, so they can react.
  • Water is liquid across the range of temperatures found on most of Earth's surface.
  • Water itself takes part in reactions, e.g. hydrolysis (water used to split molecules) and condensation (water released when molecules join).
💡 Exam tip

"Medium" means the substance in which something happens. If asked why water is the medium for life, link it to dissolving reactants so they can collide.

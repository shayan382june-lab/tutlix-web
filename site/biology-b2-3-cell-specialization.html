⚡ B2.3 in 60 seconds

  1. A zygote's cells differentiate by expressing different genes.
  2. Stem cells: self-renewal + potency (totipotent → pluripotent → multipotent).
  3. Stem cell niches: bone marrow, hair follicles.
  4. Cell size suits function; SA:V falls as cells grow, limiting size.
  5. Biconcave RBCs and microvilli increase SA:V.
  6. HL: pneumocytes, cardiac vs striated muscle, sperm vs egg.

B2.3.1

From one cell to many types

After fertilisation, the zygote divides by mitosis to form a ball of unspecialised cells. These then differentiate: different genes are switched on in different cells, so they develop different structures and functions.

C1.1.1 – C1.1.3

Enzymes and metabolism

Enzymes are biological catalysts: they speed up reactions without being used up. Metabolism is the complex network of interdependent, enzyme-controlled reactions in a cell; each step usually has its own enzyme.

🏗️ Anabolism

Building larger molecules from smaller ones, using energy. e.g. protein synthesis, photosynthesis, glycogen formation (condensation reactions).

💥 Catabolism

Breaking larger molecules into smaller ones, releasing energy. e.g. digestion, cell respiration (hydrolysis reactions).

🧠 Memory trick

"Anabolic = Assemble · Catabolic = Cut"

Anabolism builds up; catabolism breaks down.

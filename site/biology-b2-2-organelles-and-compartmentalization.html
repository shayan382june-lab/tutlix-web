B2.2.1

Organelles

An organelle is a discrete structure within a cell, adapted to carry out a specific function. Most are surrounded by membranes (e.g. nucleus, mitochondria), but ribosomes have no membrane.

Some cell parts are not usually counted as organelles: the cell wall (outside the plasma membrane), the cytoplasm (the whole contents) and the cytoskeleton (a network running through the whole cell).

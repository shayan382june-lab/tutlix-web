Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Tutlix ("we", "us"), operated by Shayan Ahmed, collects when you use tutlix.com, how we use it and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: when you request a trial lesson, we collect your name, email address, subject, level, time zone and message.
  • Purchases: payments are processed by our reseller and Merchant of Record, Paddle.com. Paddle collects your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email address, country and subscription status, but never your full card details.
  • Access codes: when you unlock members content, we store your access code, the date it was last used and a random device identifier, so we can limit each code to two devices.
  • Stored on your device: your progress in the question bank, your access code and a random device identifier are saved in your browser's local storage. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider (Netlify) automatically processes basic technical data such as IP address and browser type to deliver the website and keep it secure.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the website and members access, and to prevent sharing of access codes.
  • To reply to trial lesson requests and support messages.
  • To manage memberships and handle billing questions with Paddle.
  • To improve our content and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run Tutlix: Netlify (website hosting and form handling), Paddle (payments and subscriptions) and our email provider (to reply to you). These providers process data on our behalf and according to their own privacy commitments.

4. How long we keep information

We keep trial requests and support emails for as long as needed to help you, and membership records for as long as your membership is active plus any period required for accounting or legal purposes. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

5. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it, by emailing tutlix.ceo@gmail.com. If you live in the EU, UK or another region with data protection laws, you also have the rights given by those laws, including complaining to your local data protection authority.

6. Children

Tutlix is designed for IB students, who are usually 15 to 19 years old. If you are under the age required by the law of your country to consent to online services, please use Tutlix with the permission of a parent or guardian. Parents may contact us to review or delete their child's information.

7. Security

We use secure HTTPS connections, encrypt members content and keep admin tools password-protected. No online service can be completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page.

9. Contact

Email: tutlix.ceo@gmail.com

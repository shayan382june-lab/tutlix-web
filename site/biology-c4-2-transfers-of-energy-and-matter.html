⚡ C4.2 in 60 seconds

  1. Ecosystems are open systems; sunlight powers most of them.
  2. Food chains/webs show energy flow; decomposers feed on dead matter.
  3. Autotrophs (photo/chemo) and heterotrophs; all release energy by respiration.
  4. Only ~10% of energy passes up each level → energy pyramids, few trophic levels.
  5. Primary production is highest in tropical rainforests.
  6. Carbon cycle: photosynthesis, respiration, decomposition, combustion; the Keeling curve shows rising CO₂.

Ecosystems, energy and food chains

  • An ecosystem is a community plus its abiotic environment. Ecosystems are open systems: energy and matter can enter and leave.
  • Sunlight is the main energy source for most ecosystems; producers convert it to chemical energy (some deep-sea vent ecosystems instead rely on chemical energy).
  • Food chains show the flow of chemical energy: arrows point from food to feeder. Food webs show all the interlinked chains in a community.
  • Decomposers get their energy from carbon compounds in dead organisms, faeces and shed parts.

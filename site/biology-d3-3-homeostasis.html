D3.3.1 – D3.3.2

Homeostasis and negative feedback

  • Homeostasis: maintaining the internal environment within narrow limits, e.g. body temperature, blood pH, blood glucose, blood water potential (osmotic concentration).
  • Negative feedback: a change from the set point is detected by receptors and triggers responses that reverse the change, returning the variable to the set point.
  • This needs receptors, a coordination centre (often the hypothalamus or pancreas) and effectors (muscles or glands).

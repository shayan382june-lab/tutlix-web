⚡ B4.2 in 60 seconds

  1. A niche = habitat + way of feeding + interactions.
  2. Obligate aerobes, facultative anaerobes and obligate anaerobes differ in their use of O₂.
  3. Nutrition: autotrophs (photo/chemo), holozoic, mixotrophs, saprotrophs; archaea do all of these.
  4. Teeth reveal diet: Paranthropus (herbivore) vs Homo sapiens (omnivore).
  5. Herbivores/plants and predators/prey evolve adaptations against each other.
  6. HL: realized niche ⊂ fundamental niche; competitive exclusion.

B4.2.1

The ecological niche

Every species plays a particular role in its ecosystem: its ecological niche. A niche includes where it lives (its habitat), how it obtains food and energy, and its interactions with other species and the abiotic environment (e.g. what eats it, what it competes with, the conditions it tolerates).

🧠 Memory trick

A habitat is an address; a niche is a job

Where a species lives versus what it does there.

D4.2.1 – D4.2.4

Stable ecosystems

  • Stability: an ecosystem's ability to persist and resist or recover from disturbance. Some have lasted millions of years (e.g. tropical rainforests).
  • Requirements: a continuous energy supply, recycling of nutrients, genetic diversity, and climate within the tolerance of the species.
  • Tipping points: beyond a critical threshold, an ecosystem changes to a new state, e.g. Amazon rainforest → savanna.
  • Keystone species have a disproportionate effect on community structure.

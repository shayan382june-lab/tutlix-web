What photosynthesis does

Photosynthesis transforms light energy into chemical energy stored in carbon compounds. It is the energy source for almost every food chain.

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂  (light, chlorophyll)

  • Water is split (photolysis) to provide hydrogen (electrons + H⁺) used to reduce CO₂ to glucose.
  • Oxygen is released as a by-product of splitting water, in plants, algae and cyanobacteria.
  • Photosynthesis is anabolic and needs energy; it happens in chloroplasts.
🧠 Memory trick

O₂ comes from water, not from CO₂

Experiments with heavy oxygen (¹⁸O) proved the O₂ released comes from H₂O.

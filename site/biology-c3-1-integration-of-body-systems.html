⚡ C3.1 in 60 seconds

  1. Organisms are a hierarchy of integrated subsystems.
  2. Integration by nerves (fast, targeted) and hormones (slow, widespread).
  3. Brain integrates; spinal cord handles reflexes; nerves carry sensory + motor fibres.
  4. Hormones: melatonin (sleep), adrenaline (fight or flight), hypothalamus → pituitary.
  5. Medulla controls heart rate and ventilation using baroreceptors and chemoreceptors.
  6. HL: phototropism, auxin, cytokinin, ethylene positive feedback.

C3.1.1 – C3.1.3

Integrating a body

A multicellular organism is a hierarchy of subsystems: cells → tissues → organs → organ systems → organism. Each level has emergent properties not found in the levels below. For the body to work as a whole, systems must be integrated (coordinated).

⚡ Nervous signalling

Electrical impulses along neurons: very fast, short-lived, targeted at specific cells.

🧪 Hormonal signalling

Hormones carried in the blood: slower, longer-lasting, can affect many target cells all over the body.

The blood also integrates organs by transporting materials and heat between them.

Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

14-day money-back guarantee. If you are not happy with your Tutlix membership, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your first payment, no questions asked.

1. How to request a refund

Our orders are processed by our reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record. To request a refund, reply to your Paddle receipt email, visit paddle.net to find your order, or email us at tutlix.ceo@gmail.com with the email address you used to purchase. Refunds are returned to your original payment method.

2. Renewals

  • Monthly plans: you can cancel at any time to stop future payments. If you were charged for a renewal you did not intend, contact us within 7 days of the charge and we will refund it.
  • Yearly plans: you can request a refund within 14 days of each yearly renewal.

3. Cancelling

You can cancel your subscription at any time using the link in your Paddle receipt email or by contacting us. After cancelling, you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

4. After a refund

When a refund is made, your access code is deactivated and members-only content will lock again.

5. Your legal rights

This policy does not affect any rights you have under the consumer laws of your country.

⚡ B3.2 in 60 seconds

  1. Arteries (thick, elastic), veins (valves, wide lumen), capillaries (one cell thick).
  2. Pulse = pressure wave in arteries; count beats per minute.
  3. Plaque in coronary arteries can block blood flow → heart attack.
  4. Water rises in xylem by evaporation, cohesion and adhesion; lignin stops collapse.
  5. Stems: ring of vascular bundles; roots: central star of xylem.
  6. HL: tissue fluid & lymph · double circulation · heart & cardiac cycle · root pressure · phloem.

B3.2.1 – B3.2.5

Blood vessels

ArteryVeinCapillary
Carries bloodAway from heart at high pressureBack to heart at low pressureBetween arterioles and venules
WallThick: smooth muscle + elastic fibres + collagenThin, little muscleOne layer of endothelium
LumenNarrowWideAbout 1 red blood cell
ValvesNoYesNo
  • Arteries: elastic fibres stretch with each surge of blood and recoil, keeping blood moving between beats; muscle and collagen let the wall withstand high pressure.
  • Veins: valves stop backflow; nearby skeletal muscles squeeze veins to push blood towards the heart.
  • Capillaries: very thin walls, sometimes with pores (fenestrations), and huge numbers give a large surface area for exchange.
🧠 Memory trick

"Arteries = Away"

Arteries carry blood Away from the heart; veins bring it back (in).

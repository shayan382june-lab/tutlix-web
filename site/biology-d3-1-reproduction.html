⚡ D3.1 in 60 seconds

  1. Sexual reproduction: meiosis + fertilisation → variation.
  2. Menstrual cycle: FSH, LH, oestrogen, progesterone; negative and positive feedback.
  3. IVF uses hormones to collect eggs and fertilise them outside the body.
  4. Flowers: stamen (male), carpel (female); cross-pollination, self-incompatibility.
  5. Seeds need water, oxygen, warmth to germinate.
  6. HL: gametogenesis, polyspermy prevention, hCG, placenta, oxytocin positive feedback.

D3.1.1 – D3.1.3

Sexual and asexual reproduction

AsexualSexual
ParentsOneUsually two
GametesNoneHaploid gametes fuse (fertilisation)
OffspringGenetically identical (clones)Genetically varied
AdvantageFast; good in stable environmentsVariation helps survival in changing environments
  • Meiosis halves the chromosome number to make haploid gametes; fertilisation restores the diploid number. Both generate variation.
  • Male and female gametes differ: sperm are small, motile and numerous; eggs are large (with food reserves), non-motile and few.

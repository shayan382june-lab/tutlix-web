  3. Analogous structures come from convergent evolution.
  4. Speciation needs reproductive isolation + differential selection.

A4.1.1

What is evolution?

📘 Definition

Evolution is a change in the heritable characteristics of a population over time.

  • Only heritable (genetic) characteristics count. Features gained during a lifetime, like big muscles from training, are not passed on, so they are not evolution.
  • Populations evolve, not individuals.
⚠️ Common mistake

Avoid saying organisms evolve "because they need to" or "in order to" survive. Variation arises randomly by mutation; selection then acts on it.
